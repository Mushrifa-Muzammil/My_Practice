## 📖 About The Project

This is my **personal HTML portfolio website** showcasing my professional profile as an aspiring IT professional. The portfolio highlights:

- My **educational background** (BIT at University of Moratuwa, Cybersecurity & AI at Mars Tech)
- **Technical skills** and proficiency levels
- **Project showcase** (placeholder for future projects)
- Professional **summary** and career aspirations

This portfolio serves as a digital resume and a platform to demonstrate my growing expertise in web development and cybersecurity.
